Medical questionnaire

The purpose of this questionnaire is to determine if you should be examined by your doctor before participating in freediving activities. A positive (i.e. “YES”) response to a question does not necessarily disqualify you from freediving, but means that there is a pre-existing condition that may affect your safety while freediving, and you MUST seek the advice of a physician prior to engaging in freediving activities. The physician must sign at the bottom of the form to state that they find no medical conditions incompatible with freediving if any “YES” box is ticked. Please answer the following questions about your past and present medical history by ticking the box marked YES or NO. If you are not sure, answer YES. Do you currently or have you previously suffered from these medical conditions?
Medical history
Neurological conditions
Especially any history of seizure disorder, stroke, brain surgery, repeated blackouts or fainting fits, severe migraine headaches or aneurysm of the brain blood vessels.
Your instructor will follow up with you about this.
Cardiovascular conditions
Especially heart attack, heart surgery, irregular heartbeat, uncontrolled elevated blood pressure.
Your instructor will follow up with you about this.
Pulmonary conditions
Especially a history of spontaneous collapsed lung, collapsed lung due to injury, cysts or air pockets of the lungs, severe damage to lung tissue, emphysema, or any lung problem which interferes with your ability to breathe.
Your instructor will follow up with you about this.
Ear conditions
Permanent holes of the eardrums, history of ruptured eardrum, permanent tubes in eardrums or major ear surgery.
Your instructor will follow up with you about this.
Sinus conditions
Tumor, polyps, or cyst of the sinus cavities or nasal passages, major sinus surgery, or sinus infection.
Your instructor will follow up with you about this.
Asthma
History of asthma or asthma attacks. Any history of wheezing caused by exercise, anxiety, cold, fatigue, etc. Any condition requiring medication and/or use of an inhaler for control of wheezing.
Your instructor will follow up with you about this.
Diabetes mellitus
Especially Type I Diabetes (Insulin dependent) or Type II Diabetes, which requires insulin or oral medication for control. Any form of Diabetes that is unstable, “brittle” or produces episodes of hypoglycemia, hyperglycemia or if there is related kidney disease, eye disease, heart disease or blood vessel disease.
Your instructor will follow up with you about this.
Pregnancy
If you are currently pregnant.
Your instructor will follow up with you about this.
Freediving / scuba diving conditions
Suffered a barotrauma (pressure-related injury) in the past 4 weeks.
Your instructor will follow up with you about this.
Medication
Any medication taken on a regular basis, either over-the-counter or prescribed by a physician.
Your instructor will follow up with you about this.
General medical problems
Any physical and/or emotional condition not mentioned that might affect your safety in an underwater environment or affect your judgment under times of physical or emotional stress.
Your instructor will follow up with you about this.
